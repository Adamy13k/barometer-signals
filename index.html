Method. A 122-member multi-model ensemble is pulled for each market city. The forecast distribution is converted to a probability for the market's exact threshold/range, shrunk toward the market, and compared to the live order book. Edge thresholds widen with ensemble spread and time-to-resolution. Sizing is fractional Kelly against real book depth, with slippage and fees modelled on every fill.
